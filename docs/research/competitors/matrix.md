# Competitor comparison — 24 benchmarks

Reviewed 2026-10-04. These are different kinds of benchmarks, not 24 verified best sellers. Public evidence supports the described flows; logged-in native behavior and clinical accuracy were not tested.

## Structured · Planning

**Why it is included:** Scale reported by vendor; close daily-planning fit. **Performance evidence:** 15M+ downloads, 500K+ Pro users: vendor claims, date/definitions unspecified.

**Operation:** Capture untimed work in Inbox, place sessions on a timeline, complete and revisit the day.

**Interface:** Vertical time axis, compact day strip, colored task circles, persistent add control.

**Commercial model:** Free + Pro; monthly/yearly/lifetime.

**Stoic Body proposal:** A readable Today timeline with unscheduled capture. Add capacity explanations, linked goals, recovery and undo; do not inherit feature-parity assumptions.

Evidence: [CR05](https://structured.app/), [CR06](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/380546), [CR07](https://help.structured.app/en/articles/331330), [CR50](https://web.structured.app/). Inspection: Visual + documentation + signup boundary.

## Tiimo · Planning

**Why it is included:** Independent editorial recognition. **Performance evidence:** Apple iPhone App of the Year 2025; award, not sales rank.

**Operation:** Break projects into tasks, plan a visual day, use a focused timer.

**Interface:** Color/emoji task blocks, a day agenda, timer with subtasks, separate trophy view.

**Commercial model:** Paid offer/price not verified in this audit.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Progressive questions and one focused action view. Keep accessibility and reduce decorative density; AI suggestions require review.

Evidence: [CR04](https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1847760015), [CR48](https://www.tiimoapp.com/). Inspection: Visual + documentation.

## Todoist · Planning

**Why it is included:** Task-capture and cross-device specialist. **Performance evidence:** Not ranked as a sales leader in this audit.

**Operation:** Quickly capture a task with date/attributes, organize projects and choose list/board/calendar views.

**Interface:** Compact task rows and quick-add entry.

**Commercial model:** Free Beginner; Pro subscription.

**Stoic Body proposal:** A global capture box with visible date/goal chips. Preview ambiguous date parsing and distinguish due date from scheduled time.

Evidence: [CR08](https://www.todoist.com/pricing), [CR09](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-task-quick-add-in-todoist-va4Lhpzz). Inspection: Documentation.

## Motion · Planning

**Why it is included:** Automatic scheduling specialist. **Performance evidence:** No comparable audited sales or active-user metric verified.

**Operation:** Use task constraints to place work into available calendar time and revise the plan.

**Interface:** Calendar and task agenda; account UI not exercised.

**Commercial model:** Subscription; exact billing/segment price unresolved.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Capacity-aware scheduling and an obvious next task. Require a proposed change list before moving locked or user-controlled commitments.

Evidence: [CR10](https://www.usemotion.com/help/time-management/auto-scheduling), [CR11](https://www.usemotion.com/pricing?gc_id=22231657338&method=individual&r=0). Inspection: Documentation.

## Sunsama · Planning

**Why it is included:** Daily planning and workload specialist. **Performance evidence:** Vendor business claims are not a measured market ranking.

**Operation:** Review yesterday, select tasks, check workload, arrange/timebox and begin.

**Interface:** Guided planning ritual with workload and shutdown-time feedback.

**Commercial model:** $22/month or $204/year USD.

**Stoic Body proposal:** A short morning plan and achievable workday. Offer a fast path for busy days; incorporate family, meals and recovery in capacity.

Evidence: [CR12](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/daily-planning/), [CR13](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/billing/pricing-manifesto/). Inspection: Documentation.

## Habitica · Game

**Why it is included:** Closest life-as-RPG benchmark. **Performance evidence:** Specialist relevance; current revenue rank unverified.

**Operation:** Complete habits/dailies/tasks to gain XP, gold and items; quests add accountability.

**Interface:** Separate task columns, avatar statistics, inventory and rewards.

**Commercial model:** Game economy present; current paid price not verified.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Campaigns, attributes, personal rewards and milestones. Omit damage for missed habits and pressure on party members; keep rewards tied to sustainable behavior.

Evidence: [CR14](https://habitica.com/static/features), [CR15](https://habitica.com/static/faq). Inspection: Visual + documentation.

## Finch · Game

**Why it is included:** Chart presence and large store-rating sample. **Performance evidence:** US free chart 11; indexed listing 12 with 757K ratings; rendered listing 10 with 759K ratings. Both listings 4.9. Snapshots differ.

**Operation:** Select small goals, earn energy through care, send pet on an adventure and return to its story.

**Interface:** Warm companion theme and a small-action loop; native tap sequence not tested.

**Commercial model:** Free core; optional Finch Plus.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Supportive return loop, customizable goals, celebration of small effort. Translate emotional progress into an optional Stoic journey; no compulsory pet or guilt prompts.

Evidence: [CR01](https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/charts/6013?chart=top-free), [CR16](https://finchcare.com/about-finch), [CR17](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37935669335309-Our-Approach-to-Self-Care), [CR47](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finch-self-care-pet/id1528595748). Inspection: Visual + documentation.

## Fabulous · Game

**Why it is included:** Routine and coaching specialist. **Performance evidence:** Specialist relevance; not verified as a top seller.

**Operation:** Start with a desired life change; follow routines and use coaching resources.

**Interface:** Motivation-first landing questionnaire and themed coaching content.

**Commercial model:** Current exact offer not verified.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Small routines and practical coaching prompts. Avoid long setup before the first useful day; every prompt should lead to a concrete action.

Evidence: [CR18](https://www.thefabulous.co/landing/). Inspection: Documentation.

## Duolingo · Learning

**Why it is included:** Company-reported paid and active-user scale. **Performance evidence:** Q4 2025: 52.7M DAU, 133.1M MAU, 12.2M paid subscribers.

**Operation:** Repeat small learning sessions; streaks/freeze mechanics encourage return.

**Interface:** A prominent continuity/progress mechanic; current native lesson screens not inspected.

**Commercial model:** Free and paid subscriber business; local current price not verified.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Short learning quests, progress visibility and grace days. Practice evidence determines skill mastery; XP and streaks do not certify physical readiness.

Evidence: [CR03](https://investors.duolingo.com/company-strategy-overview-0), [CR19](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/). Inspection: Documentation.

## MyFitnessPal · Nutrition

**Why it is included:** Chart and historical revenue-estimate evidence. **Performance evidence:** US free chart 10; included in Sensor Tower US Q4 2025 revenue leaders.

**Operation:** Log food through several entry modes; inspect targets; paid meal plans produce shopping support.

**Interface:** Food diary plus summary and meal-planning flows.

**Commercial model:** Premium $79.99/year; Plus $99.99/year or $24.99/month USD.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Saved meals, editable fast capture, recipes and groceries. Make source/portion corrections obvious; explain paid boundaries before users invest effort.

Evidence: [CR01](https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/charts/6013?chart=top-free), [CR02](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-health-and-fitness-revenue-us-600af518241bc16eb8dce802), [CR20](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/premium?source=home_page), [CR21](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032625951-MyFitnessPal-Premium-features). Inspection: Documentation.

## Cronometer · Nutrition

**Why it is included:** Chart presence and detailed nutrient specialty. **Performance evidence:** US free chart 20; no audited paying-user count verified.

**Operation:** Log food and inspect energy/macros/micronutrients; Gold adds richer charts and import.

**Interface:** Summary bars/rings with deeper nutrient detail; prominent diary add action.

**Commercial model:** Free Basic; Gold displayed $10.99 USD/month.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Nutrient source labels and detail on demand. Use a simple daily summary first; do not equate missing nutrient data with zero.

Evidence: [CR01](https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/charts/6013?chart=top-free), [CR22](https://cronometer.com/features/), [CR23](https://cronometer.com/gold/). Inspection: Visual + documentation.

## MacroFactor · Nutrition

**Why it is included:** Adaptive planning and bundled workout competitor. **Performance evidence:** Vendor product page says 500K users; activity/payment definitions unknown.

**Operation:** Log intake and weight; estimated expenditure informs periodic target updates.

**Interface:** Strategy/check-in screen, trend view and explanations of program changes.

**Commercial model:** Single app $71.99/year or $11.99/month; qualifying bundle $89.99/year USD.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Weekly review, trend-based estimates and explanations. Require sufficient reliable data; use independently specified formulas and show uncertainty.

Evidence: [CR24](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/20-expenditure), [CR25](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/393-how-macrofactor-subscriptions-and-bundles-work), [CR26](https://macrofactor.com/adherence-neutral/), [CR49](https://macrofactor.com/macrofactor/). Inspection: Visual + documentation.

## Lose It! · Nutrition

**Why it is included:** Nutrition incumbent; upgrade-flow comparison. **Performance evidence:** Not assigned a revenue rank here.

**Operation:** Food tracking continues through a free-to-Premium upgrade with prior account history retained.

**Interface:** Exact native screens uninspected; documentation establishes upgrade continuity.

**Commercial model:** Variable promotional pricing; current universal price cannot be stated.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Preserve history and favorites across paid/free transitions. Offer transparent, stable terms; avoid deriving causal effectiveness from subscriber comparisons.

Evidence: [CR27](https://loseit.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/47345136549652-What-is-Lose-It-Premium). Inspection: Documentation.

## Zero · Nutrition

**Why it is included:** Meal-timing specialist relevant to OMAD preference. **Performance evidence:** Vendor number-one claim not treated as ranking evidence.

**Operation:** Start/edit an eating-window timer and review food/protein and timing history.

**Interface:** Large fasting timer and related daily nutrition summaries.

**Commercial model:** Try-free/download offer; current price not verified.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Optional timing controls and editable timestamps. Exclude fasting-duration XP and unsupported physiological countdowns.

Evidence: [CR28](https://zerolongevity.com/). Inspection: Documentation.

## Hevy · Fitness

**Why it is included:** Workout-logging specialist. **Performance evidence:** No independently verified sales rank.

**Operation:** Start a routine, compare previous sets, enter reps/load, complete sets, rest and save.

**Commercial model:** Free + Pro; four routine limit documented for free tier.

**Stoic Body proposal:** Low-friction set logging and previous-performance context. Keep a live workout recoverable offline; social sharing optional and private by default.
