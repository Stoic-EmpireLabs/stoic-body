# Phase 1 research agenda

Status: initial Phase 1 review completed; see [review packet](phase-1-review.md), [37-source register](research/sources.md), [health review](research/health-and-training.md) and [platform review](research/platform-and-launch.md). This agenda records intended coverage. Production diet cards, quantitative formulas, exercise protocols, launch-jurisdiction obligations and device execution checks remain for their approved phases.

## Evidence record

For each claim retain: question, exact supporting source, publisher, publication/update date, retrieval date, study type/population where relevant, outcome, uncertainty, conflicts/limitations, applicability and next review date. Keep research evidence separate from personal health data.

Prefer authoritative public health guidance and peer-reviewed research. Use primary research for technical/clinical claims where suitable; syntheses can contextualize the strength of the wider evidence. Verify that each source supports the actual displayed claim.

## Nutrition

Research each required pattern independently:
balanced; Mediterranean; higher-protein; vegetarian; vegan; low-carb; ketogenic; intermittent fasting/time-restricted eating; OMAD; carnivore; prescribed clear/full liquid; complete meal replacements; commercial liquid programs.

Evaluate evidence quality, effectiveness by relevant outcome, nutritional adequacy, training fit, practicality/cost, adherence, exclusions and circumstances requiring professional oversight. Distinguish timing, food selection and energy intake. Do not manufacture symmetry when the evidence base differs.

Calculation work: identify suitable estimation methods, applicable population, units, uncertainty, excluded populations and minimum input requirements before deciding formulas. Investigate handling of sparse/noisy measurements and energy-expenditure estimates.

## Exercise and progress

Research appropriate program selection and progression for every requested training mode. Separate resistance intensity concepts from HIIT. Review exercise technique sources, substitution criteria, recovery and deload approaches. Define which metrics are measured versus estimated and how to handle discomfort or restrictions.

## Platform and alarms

After the device answer, use current official platform documentation to compare responsive web/PWA, native and possible hybrid approaches. Evaluate foreground/background/closed-app reminders, locked device, reboot, denied permission, battery restrictions, timezone/DST, offline operation, local storage, backup and multi-device limitations. Verify current platform behavior; do not rely on remembered browser capabilities.

Compare implementation and maintenance effort within the zero-paid-services constraint. A local desktop database alone is not a mobile sync solution.

## Resources and quotations

Define checks for real, relevant tutorial/video links, accessibility alternatives, level and cost. For quotations, retain reliable author/work/location and edition/translation where appropriate; label paraphrases and original reflections.

## Commercial product and distribution

After Phase 0 approval, research current official Apple, Google Play and Microsoft Store requirements for the selected devices, health-related claims, privacy disclosures, purchases/entitlements, account deletion where applicable, age ratings, signing, packaging, review and developer enrollment costs. Record verification dates; do not assume three stores share requirements or that a web app satisfies native alarm requirements.

Compare one-time purchase, subscription and free-plus-paid features against recurring support, content and any AI costs. No model is chosen. Propose positioning and marketing channels with source-backed research. Any analytics should be optional and minimize health-data collection. Review jurisdiction-specific obligations only after intended launch markets and data flows are known.

The inherited zero-paid-services constraint is still active. If commercial requirements need paid enrollment or infrastructure, present the concrete cost and alternative before any purchase or account creation.
