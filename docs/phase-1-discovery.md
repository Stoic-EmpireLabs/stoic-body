# Phase 1 discovery readiness

Date: 2026-10-04.

## Confirmed product context

The owner supplied the product intent directly: enjoyable and easy daily use; owner-first pilot of a commercial app; first-release iPhone/iPad and Windows PC targets; eventual Apple, Google and Microsoft store distribution; coordinated health/life/learning/game features; and phase approvals.

The personal profile is intentionally in private/. It includes desired routine, equipment, goals and the latest boxing preference. It is an input to later reviewed setup, not public demo data or an implemented app import.

## Latest additions

- Boxing: home shadowboxing, footwork and fitness; bag work conditional on equipment availability.
- Family learning: learn appropriate support/lifting skills to help a daughter become a cheer flyer. Proposed route uses qualified coaching and supervised progression; child age, coach access and skill readiness are unknown.
- Academic planning: user intends to upload their DBA schedule inside the future app. Dates, recurring sessions and deadlines need reviewed extraction before scheduling.

## What the current information resolves

- The app needs equipment-aware training alternatives and coordinated workload.
- The planner needs family time as a protected category and school deadlines as confirmed imported constraints.
- A health plan cannot be inferred from a photo, an appearance goal or game level.
- The product must accommodate unfinished onboarding without inventing personal facts.
- Business development, learning, maintenance, home tasks and digital organization are first-class goal types rather than fitness-only exceptions.
- The user wants minimal friction; ask subsequent questions only when an answer changes the current decision.

## Evidence boundary

There is no customer analytics, sales or retention dataset yet. The owner's goals are strong product requirements but not proof of market demand. The data-analytics business skills were evaluated; metric-backed analysis is deferred until suitable pilot/business data exists. No unrelated email, calendar or business connector was inspected.

The Phase 1 decision-comparator workflow uses the same criteria across platform and monetization options and labels unknowns. Health research is a scoped review with source/access limits, not a systematic review or personal clinical assessment.
