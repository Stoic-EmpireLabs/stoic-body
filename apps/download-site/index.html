YOUR HOST, FROM THE FIRST OPEN

where to start.

The welcome guide introduces the app, asks questions at your pace, and walks you through your workspace.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Extract the ZIP, then run Install Stoic Body.cmd. Your desktop shortcut opens a dedicated app window.

  2. 2

    Create your own account

    Save your recovery key, answer what you know, and skip what you want to come back to. Each person has a separate account.

  3. 3

    Your first week, ready to start

    Choose bulk, cut, lean out or maintain. Finish the multiple-choice setup and start from your automatically saved week. Help & tour stays available.

YOUR DATA, ON YOUR COMPUTER

Close it. Come back. Continue.

Saved progress stays in a local database. You can shut down your PC. Optional private Windows sync queues changes while the host is off and catches up when both apps are connected through Tailscale.

Encrypted account backups are available in Settings. Photos use a separate encrypted archive under Health → Photos & goal. Local Windows files remain readable to someone with access to your account on the computer.

A working preview, with clear limits.

This site distributes the desktop app; it does not host your private account. Personal photo upload and comparison work locally. AI goal-image rendering is not enabled yet. Apple offline apps, native alarms and Store releases remain in development.

Closing the window leaves a small local service running. Use the included Stop launcher or shut down your PC. Private sync needs a reachable host and working Tailscale DNS.
